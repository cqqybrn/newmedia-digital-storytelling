## Slide 18

- North Korean Refugee
- A humanitarian term under international law (UNHCR framework) for individuals fleeing persecution or severe human rights abuses in North Korea.
- Human rights, forced displacement, and protection needs rather than political ideology.
- 탈북자 (Talbukja): Literally translating to “defector from the North.”
- 새터민 (Saeteomin): Introduced by the South Korean government in 2005 as a more welcoming alternative, this term means “people of a new land.”
- 북한이탈주민 (Bukhan Ital-jumin): This is the official legal and administrative term used by the South Korean government in laws, policy documents, and media. It translates neutrally to “North Korean defected residents” or “North Korean escapee residents.”
