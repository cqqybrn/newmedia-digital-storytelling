## 第 7 页

Group 1: The Legal Limbo & 
Procedural Barriers
• Focus Area: Legal framework (Refugee Act of 2013, non-referral 
decisions, non-refoulement, transit zone ambiguity).
• Preparatory Research Project:
• "The Flowchart of Exclusion": Map the legal journey of an asylum 
seeker arriving at Incheon Airport versus standard passport control. 
They analyze court precedents (2021 Incheon District Court 
corrective ruling allowing transit applications) alongside the UN 
Committee’s findings on arbitrary detention.
• Roblox Game Translation:
• Mechanic — The In-Between Zone: A spatial puzzle/narrative 
game set inside a stylized airport terminal. Players interact with 
legal choices, administrative paperwork, and countdown timers 
that visually shrink the playable space as procedural delays 
mount, demonstrating how procedural gray zones feel in practice.


## 第 8 页

Group 2: Spatial Control, 
Architecture & Inhumane 
Conditions
• Focus Area: Architecture of border control, daily spatial restrictions, 
basic human rights (hygiene, privacy, light).
• Preparatory Research Project:
• "Architectural & Sensory Mapping":Based on press coverage and 
the UN decision detailing 14 months of constant artificial light, thin 
platform mattresses, and living on airline food trays, create a 
sensory and spatial blueprint of the transit departure waiting room 
versus living spaces outside.
• Roblox Game Translation:
• Mechanic — Liminal Survival: A survival/resource management 
simulation where players must manage "Dignity" , "Health" , and 
"Morale" meters while trapped within a single gated terminal node. 
The game forces reliance on dynamic mutual-aid mechanics with 
passing NPC travelers to secure basic necessities like medicine or 
fresh air (e.g., finding the small garden near Gate 283).


## 第 9 页

Group 3: Historical Precedents & 
Korean Notions of Displacement
• Focus Area: Korean historical memory of displacement (Korean War refugees, 
Hwanghae internal migrants, North Korean escapees/talbukjin) vs. modern 
foreign refugee policies.
• Preparatory Research Project:
• "Comparative Oral History Archive": Examine historical accounts of 
Korean refugees during the 1950–1953 war (e.g., the Hungnam 
Evacuation, temporary living shelters in Busan) alongside modern 
international refugee cases in Korea. They explore the cultural concept 
of jeong (정) and why it historically applied to internal displacement but 
encounters friction with foreign asylum seekers.
• Roblox Game Translation:
• Mechanic — Parallel Timelines: A dynamic story game where players 
step between two historical periods in Korea (1951 Busan refugee 
camps vs. 2026 Incheon Airport). Players solve environmental puzzles by 
identifying shared human needs and historical parallels between Korean 
refugees in the past and today’s international asylum seekers.


## 第 10 页

Group 4: Language, 
Translanguaging & Digital Media 
Literacy
• Focus Area: Communication barriers, legal translation, media framing, 
and public perception in South Korea.
• Preparatory Research Project:
• "Media Framing & Linguistic Analysis":Compare Korean news 
reporting (e.g., focusing on immigration loopholes or economic 
burdens) against global human rights reporting (e.g., UN OHCHR 
press releases). They identify key terms (namin [난민], ibguk heoga
[입국허가]) and trace how language shapes public empathy.
• Roblox Game Translation:
• Mechanic — The Lost Translation Box: A collaborative puzzle 
game where two players must communicate across a language 
barrier using symbols, limited context cards, and translated legal 
documents. One player acts as an advocate outside the terminal, 
while the other plays the detainee inside, illustrating the vital role of 
legal translation and human rights advocacy.


## 第 11 页

Group 5: The Child’s 
Rights & Family 
Perspective
• Focus Area: UN Convention on the Rights of the Child, education rights, 
family detention (drawing on recent 2026 cases like the West African 
family held at Incheon).
• Preparatory Research Project:
• "The Right to Childhood Audit": Analyze Article 28 (Right to 
Education) and Article 31 (Right to Rest and Play) under 
international law, evaluating how extended airport detention 
impacts childhood development and emotional stability.
• Roblox Game Translation:
• Mechanic — Through Younger Eyes: An exploratory adventure 
game told through the eyes of a child navigating an adult-
dominated airport space. The game uses visual imagination 
mechanics—turning drab waiting room platforms and boarding 
gates into fantastical worlds—highlighting the contrast between 
a child's resilience and their harsh structural reality.


## 第 12 页

Group 6: Civil Society, NGO 
Networks & Public Advocacy
• Focus Area: Public interest legal groups in South Korea (e.g., Duroo, 
NAMIN), civil activism, and international pressure.
• Preparatory Research Project:
• "Advocacy Strategy Brief":Examine how Korean human rights 
lawyers and NGOs brought the Incheon case to the UN Human 
Rights Committee. They map out the network of support required 
to assist a single refugee (legal defense, medical assistance, 
translation, international reporting).
• Roblox Game Translation:
• Mechanic — Advocate's Quest: A strategy/role-playing game 
where players manage an NGO team in Seoul. Players balance 
gathering evidence, navigating legal petitions in Korean courts, 
organizing public awareness campaigns, and providing direct aid to 
individuals stuck at ports of entry.
