ASMR TOWER DISCUSSION

Pros:

Most players in the game play to feel satisfied by the asmr because squishies/wax/& asmr overall are trending among children and young adults.

Attention-span

Incorporating sound effects in the game to amplify scenes

(ex: heartbeats, loud noise, basically all the sounds you would hear at an airport to show how overwhelming the experience can be firsthand)

When traversing space, we can deform materials within the space in a manner similar to changes in the user's (asylum seeker’s) emotional state.

ex) Not approved -> Solid material

Approved -> Comfy material

In ASMR Tower, players fall when they make a mistake and have to restart from the nearest checkpoint. This game mechanic can be connected to the uncertainty refugees face during their journey.

Failing to cross a border, facing difficulties with documents, or being denied asylum could cause players to return to an earlier stage of the game.

Players can experience making choices firsthand.

Usually, there’s an incentive at the end of obby’s like this, so it pushes the player to reach the end + knowing that they’ll receive a reward.

Cons:

We couldn’t pinpoint the correlation between the game and refugees

Too many sound effects can distract the player's attention.

There is a risk of turning refugees' suffering into entertainment.

It may reduce serious human rights issues to a simple game experience.

It may create stereotypes about refugees.
