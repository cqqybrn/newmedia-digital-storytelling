2nd GROUP PROJECT – Front Row 
ASMR Tower Discussion 
Pros 
The game’s overall layout, design, and aesthetic are filled with many vibrant colors. The 
different noises after each jump and interaction adds a sense of immersion and relaxation to the 
experience. Not only is the atmosphere calming, but the gameplay depicted is very 
straightforward, that of being an archetype of a climbing and parkour game. An individual is able 
to pick up these mechanics fast after playing just a few minutes of the game. 
The simplicity of ASMR Tower allows the designers and creators of the game to be pretty 
flexible with further implementation and expanding additional elements. There are many ways 
the game can add to its difficulty or through informational topics. 
The game lacks a risk factor, in the sense that when dying or failing to surpass a certain level, a 
save mechanic is implemented, allowing the player to restart from the previous stage. The lack of 
major consequences after losing lowers the tension and anxiety one could have when playing the 
game, allowing the players to focus more on the comfortable experience and environment over 
survival. 
 
Cons 
The game does not try to create a storytelling element that immerses the player into an 
adventure. The lack of including this can result in the player’s experience not being memorable 
enough to keep coming back. 
While parkour games are pretty basic, all mainly consisting of traversing obstacles to make it to 
the next levels, they lack an incentive to keep pushing, such as rewards outside of crates, or like 
mentioned before, a storytelling narrative. Repetition is prone to instilling boredom by the 
player, which can ultimately lead to them leaving the experience. 
With a game that focuses on the use of ASMR sounds and audio cues, the expansive library of 
sounds is not as impressive as the name sets it out to be. Perhaps widening the range of noises, or 
keeping ones exclusive to other towers and levels can make interactions more creative and 
unique. 
The premise of the game being to traverse a tower restricts the spatial and map innovation that 
can be designed as a result. ASMR Tower forces the design to follow a tower aesthetic, so any 
other architectural buildings and elements are unable to be explored.

ROBLOX STUDIO ENVIRONMENT PAGE 
Terminal Limbo is a storytelling game based on the story of Gentilmen Issa Magumba. It 
takes place in an airport where the player is “stuck”, or rather has no final destination, and 
remains in transit while having to make decisions about their situation. 
The environment is made from scratch but with support from tools within Roblox Studio. 
The airport environment is built with waiting areas, seating, shelves, signs, restricted doors, 
and different rooms. We also used lighting, sounds, and scripts to make the environment 
more interactive. 
 
Challenges During Development 
We encountered several problems while developing the game: 
Map issues: Because Roblox utilizes squares, making all areas fit well together without 
“escape routes” out of the map was difficult, and if you look closely, you can probably still 
find ways to escape. 
 
Game endings: 
The game has seven possible endings, but some were difficult to reach and others too easy, 
as they have not yet been balanced. 
The storylines for now are also fairly simple, but we hope to improve them through our 
storytelling writers. 
 
NPC behavior: 
Getting the security guards to behave “reasonably” in the game was another challenge. 
There were problems with their patrol routes, chasing behavior, and how they reacted to 
the player. We did not want guards to automatically chase the player without a reason, but 
rather react to suspicious or illegal activities. Making their movement and reactions work 
as intended required several adjustments. Preventing the guards from trying to sprint 
through walls also took trial and error. 
The game for now still requires a lot of bug fixing, and the current build plays more like a 
playtest rather than a final product.
