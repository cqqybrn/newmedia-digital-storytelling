- Refugee Status

- The Universal Declaration of Human Rights (Article 14): Guarantees the right to seek and enjoy asylum from persecution in other countries.
- Grounds for Entry/Stay: Fleeing persecution, war, or violence based on race, religion, nationality, political opinion, or membership in a particular social group.
